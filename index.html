About Me

I’m Daniel Phutiagae, a web developer passionate about helping businesses grow through practical, professional, and user-friendly websites.

I help South African businesses build a strong online presence with websites designed around their goals. Whether you need a professional website to showcase your services, an online store to sell your products, or a booking website that makes it easier for customers to schedule appointments, I focus on creating solutions that work for your business.

My approach is simple: your website should do more than just look good. It should make it easier for customers to find you, understand what you offer, and take the next step—whether that means making a purchase, booking a service, or contacting you.

I specialise in WordPress website development, including WooCommerce e-commerce stores and service-based websites with booking functionality. I also aim to make the process straightforward, with clear communication and solutions tailored to each client's needs.

If you're ready to take your business online or improve your existing website, let's work together.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of websites do you build?

I build professional WordPress websites for South African small businesses, including business websites, service-based websites, and e-commerce stores. Whether you want to showcase your services, sell products online, or allow customers to book appointments, I can help you find a solution that fits your business needs.

How much does a website cost?

Website pricing depends on the size, functionality, and specific requirements of your project. A simple business website will generally cost less than a full e-commerce store or a website with an online booking system. Get in touch for a quote tailored to your needs and budget.

Can you build an online store for my business?

Yes! I can build an online store using WordPress and WooCommerce, allowing you to showcase your products, manage your inventory, and accept online orders. Payment options can be integrated based on your business requirements and the supported payment providers

Can customers book appointments through my website?

Yes. I can help set up a booking website that allows customers to view your available services and schedule appointments online. The features can be tailored to your business, whether you offer consultations, personal services, or appointments.

How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends on the complexity of your website, the features required, and how quickly content and other necessary information are provided. A basic business website may take less time than an online store or a website with booking functionality. I'll discuss the expected timeline with you before the project begins.

Will I be able to update my website myself?

Yes. WordPress makes it possible to manage many aspects of your website without needing to write code. Depending on your website setup, you can update text and images, publish content, manage products, and handle other routine tasks. I can also guide you through the basics so you feel comfortable managing your website.
